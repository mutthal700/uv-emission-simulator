# ICU guideline archive verification

**Verification date:** 2026-08-29  
**Input:** `Archive.zip`  
**Scope:** ICU ventilation and room-geometry requirements requested for Australia/New Zealand, the United States, the United Kingdom, India, Germany, and optionally Canada.

## Verification rule

Only direct primary text from the named document and edition can close a comparison cell. A secondary review may corroborate a value but cannot replace the applicable standard. A state guideline, archived edition, revision summary, or addendum addressing another table is not substituted for the requested document.

Every pasted entry below uses the mandatory citation order:

1. **Document title**
2. **Edition/year**
3. **Table or section number and named row/space**
4. **Values and evidence status**

`Same as above` is deliberately not used; the revision and locator are repeated with every entry.

## Supplemental and historical evidence from the archive

### Australia - Victorian guideline, not AusHFG/AS 1668.2

**Document title:** *Engineering guidelines for healthcare facilities: Volume 4 - Heating, ventilation and air conditioning*  
**Edition/year:** Health technical guideline HTG-2020-004, Victorian Health and Human Services Building Authority, May 2020  
**Table/section:** §4.172, `ICU and CCU`, printed/PDF page 34

- Air-handling units serving the pressurised ICU patient zone incorporate remote HEPA filtration outside the ICU.
- Outside air to patient areas: 50%.
- Positive air movement is maintained from beds toward adjoining circulation space.
- Heat recovery is required to exhaust systems in line with NCC Part J.
- §4.173 refers to `Reference table 1` for other conditions, but that table is not present in the supplied PDF.

This is direct Victorian primary guidance, but it does not close the requested AusHFG Part E or AS 1668.2 comparison entry.
